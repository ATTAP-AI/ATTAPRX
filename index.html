Free brand-name drugs (Patient Assistance Programs). Manufacturer PAPs ship brand medications free or near-free to patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or who fall below ~400–500% of the Federal Poverty Level (varies by program). Major programs included here: Lilly Cares, Pfizer RxPathways, Novartis Patient Assistance Now, BMS Patient Assistance Foundation, myAbbVie Assist, Sanofi Patient Connection, Boehringer Ingelheim Cares, GSK Patient Assistance, Merck Patient Assistance, AZ&Me (AstraZeneca), Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance, Bayer Patient Assistance. Each row's Free via PAP link opens the actual program application.

Not medical advice. Talk to your prescriber and pharmacist before switching pharmacies, brands, or doses. RxPass cannot be combined with insurance, HSA, or FSA. Walmart's $4 list, Costco Member Pricing, and Cost Plus Drugs are cash-pay programs — using insurance may yield a different price. PAP eligibility usually requires a U.S. residency, an income test, and a clinician-completed application.
